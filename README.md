The Gilded Hour — a complete top-down museum heist in JavaScript/HTML5. You are Vesper, a thief slipping through three galleries at midnight: the Marble Hall, the Gilded Gallery, and the Vault of Hours. Read the guards' sweeping blue vision cones and the cameras' red ones before you move, crawl the vents, throw smoke pellets, fire an EMP to black out the cameras, and lift every treasure — then grab the Gilded Hourglass itself, which trips the vault alarm, and sprint for the getaway door before the alarm meter hits 100. Guards patrol, pause and scan, grow suspicious, chase and search; lasers blink on the final floor; a Ghost bonus rewards a run that never trips the alarm past 50. Synthesized Web Audio footsteps, alerts and chimes — no audio files, no network requests, just static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```

WASD / arrows to move, Shift to sneak, Space to dash, click or 1 to throw smoke, 2 for EMP, P to pause, M to mute, R to retry a floor, Enter to confirm on menus.
